Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Lost & Found system work? +

Students can report lost or found items through our platform. When an item is reported, it appears on the campus feed. Other students can search and match items to help reunite belongings with their owners.

How do I return a found item? +

Report it on the platform with a photo and location. You can also take the item to the nearest block office. The owner can contact you through the platform to arrange collection.

What safety tips should I follow? +

Always verify the identity of anyone claiming an item. Meet in a public campus location. Never share personal financial information. Label your belongings with your student ID for easier recovery.

Who can use this system? +

This system is exclusively for GCET students and staff. You need a valid @gcet.edu.in email address to log in. This ensures campus safety and accountability.

How long are items kept on the platform? +

Items remain listed for 30 days. If unclaimed, they are archived. Physical items held at block offices are transferred to the main admin office after 2 weeks.
